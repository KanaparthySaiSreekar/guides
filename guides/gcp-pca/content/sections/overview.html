What changed since older prep material

Most courses, dumps and blog posts online still describe the previous exam. These are the dated changes that matter.

  1. 30 Oct 2025
    New exam guide takes effectAltostrat Media, Cymbal Retail and KnightMotives Automotive replace Helicopter Racing League, Mountkirk Games and TerramEarth. EHR Healthcare stays. New AI sections 2.4 and 2.5, “Securing AI” in 3.1, and the Well-Architected Framework as a required lens. Weights moved from 24/15/18/18/11/14 to 25/17.5/17.5/15/12.5/12.5. A separately named “v6.1” PDF circulates with slightly different weights (about 25/18/19/15/11/12) and an extra objective 4.3; this guide follows the one linked from Google’s certification page.
  2. 2 Mar 2026
    Exams move to Pearson VUEKryterion’s last day was 22 Feb; the first Pearson VUE exams were on 2 Mar. Content and format are unchanged. You still register through Google’s certification portal (CM Connect), then sit at a Pearson VUE centre or online with OnVUE.
  3. 31 Mar 2026
    Deployment Manager reaches end of supportNew users are blocked from 30 Jun 2026 and the service shuts down after 30 Jun 2027. Infrastructure Manager (managed Terraform) is Google’s recommended replacement, and DM Convert turns configurations into Terraform or Kubernetes Resource Model (Config Connector) files.
  4. 22 Apr 2026
    Vertex AI becomes Gemini Enterprise Agent PlatformAnnounced at Cloud Next. The exam guide now uses the new names, for example “Agent Platform Pipelines.” More renames followed; see the table below.
  5. 9 Jul 2026
    Renewal through Google SkillsPCA holders can renew by completing designated courses or skill badges in the final year (adds 1 year), or by taking the renewal exam (adds 2 years).
  6. Sep 2026
    Professional Agentic Architect launchesA separate certification (beta exam 8–30 Sep, general availability planned for mid-November). It is a different exam from the PCA, so don’t confuse the two guides.
