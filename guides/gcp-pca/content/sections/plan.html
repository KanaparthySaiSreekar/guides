Only two weeks?

Days 1–4

  • Read all four case studies twice, then fill in each requirement-to-service table from memory.
  • Learn the cheat sheet signals and traps until you can answer with the answers hidden.
  • Do domain drills for domains 1 and 3 (42.5% of the exam).

Days 5–10

  • Drill domains 2, 4, 5 and 6, plus every case-study set.
  • Read the AI & ML and Security blocks of the cheat sheet closely.
  • Run Mistakes review every evening.

Days 11–14

  • Two full timed mocks. Review every explanation, including for questions you got right.
  • Do Google’s official sample questions (they may refer to earlier case studies, but the style holds).
  • Run the OnVUE system test and confirm your ID.
